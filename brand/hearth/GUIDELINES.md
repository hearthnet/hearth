# Hearth logo: usage

**Idea.** The H is a fireplace. Its crossbar is the mantel, and a three-tongue fire burns in the firebox below. The fire is also a small chain tree: one root branching up.

## Colours

| Name | Use | HEX | RGB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ember | The fire, accents | `#E8590C` | 232 89 12 |
| Charcoal | The H, the wordmark, dark tiles | `#2B2420` | 43 36 32 |
| Cream | The H and wordmark on dark backgrounds | `#F6EFE6` | 246 239 230 |

- **One-colour:** all charcoal, all black or all white.
- **Never** recolour the H in ember, or put ember on ember.

## Rules

- **Clear space:** one H stem's width (44/256 of the symbol's height) on every side.
- **Minimum size:** the full symbol at 32 px and up; the small cut from 16 to 31 px; the horizontal lockup at 24 px high and up.
- **Don't** add effects, stretch or rotate it, outline it, put the flame outside the H, or set "hearth" in another typeface next to the symbol.
- **The wordmark** is drawn by hand: monoline, a 28-unit stroke, lowercase. It uses no font, so there is no licence to clear.
